Things to Do

Museums, with what’s actually up during Oct 14–21, 2026. Left column is travel time from the hotel.

🗓 Closure days matter more than usual here. The Met closes Wednesdays. The Guggenheim has been closing Thursdays. The Whitney, the Morgan and the Brooklyn Museum close Mondays — which is exactly the day Dad set aside for museums. Confirm each one’s calendar the week before.

The big four

6 · 15m
The Met — Fifth Av at 82nd
Krasner and Pollock: Past Continuous (opens Oct 4) is the headline — the two of them traced side by side. Also up: Orientalism: Between Fact and Fantasy, A King’s Carpet: Louis XIV and the Savonnerie, Chasing Clouds, Si Lewen’s Parade, and Liu Wei on the facade. Closed Wednesdays.
ExhibitionsRoute
NRW·12m
MoMA — W 53rd St
The Surrealist Book: Tomorrow Lives in Mirrors (opens Oct 4). Plus Full Disclosure: The Edge of Information Design and Taking Back Our Space: Photographic Perspectives. Brancusi opens Oct 25 — four days too late for us. Open daily.
ExhibitionsRoute
6 · 18m
Guggenheim — Fifth Av at 89th
Taryn Simon: Father Country I Do Love You in the rotunda, and the big Pop survey running through January. The building is the exhibit. Has been closing Thursdays.
ExhibitionsRoute
1 · 12m
Whitney — Gansevoort St
Roy Lichtenstein: Like New opens Oct 11 — 130+ works, and it reunites all eight pieces from his 1962 Castelli debut for the first time. Also Minnie Evans: The Lost World and Coumba Samba. Normally closed Mon & Tue — do it Thursday. Walk the High Line back.
ExhibitionsRoute

Close to the hotel

8m walk
The Morgan Library & Museum — 36th & Madison
The closest serious museum to us. Hujar: Contact and Friends Who Came to See Me: Ashbery’s collection both close Oct 25, so this week is the last call. McKim’s original library room alone is worth the ticket. Closed Mondays.
Exhibitions
5m walk
Poster House — W 23rd St
Small, sharp, cheap, and five minutes away. Reading Under Fire: Arming Minds & Hearts During Wartime runs to Nov 1. A good hour when a big museum is too much.
posterhouse.org
10m walk
Empire State Building · Flatiron · Madison Sq Park
All within a ten-minute radius. The ESB observatory at dusk is the least-effort “view” option if the rooftop restaurants don’t come together.

Worth the trip

6 · 20m
The Frick Collection — 70th & Fifth
Siena: The Art of Bronze, 1450–1500 opens Oct 15, the day after we land — forty Renaissance bronzes, most never shown outside Tuscany. In the restored mansion. Open Mondays.
Exhibitions
1 · 15m
American Museum of Natural History — 79th & CPW
The Gilder Center, the blue whale, the Hayden Planetarium space show. The obvious pick if Jasung is along.
Exhibitions
23 · 35m
Brooklyn Museum — Eastern Parkway
A deep bench that week: Art of Manga and Manga Before Manga (both from Oct 3), Cézanne to Modigliani: the Pearlman Collection, Hopi Kachina Dolls, and Michael Richards: Free F’All. Closed Mon & Tue. Pair with dinner at Celestine or the River Café.
Exhibitions
6 · 22m
The Jewish Museum — 92nd & Fifth
Women of the Wiener Werkstätte (to Nov 15) and Fragments of Memory. Small, and a natural add-on to a Met or Guggenheim morning.
Exhibitions
NRW·15m
Museum of Arts and Design — Columbus Circle
Nike: Form Follows Motion, a full design survey. Ten minutes from Lincoln Center — stackable with the opera day.
Exhibitions
6 · 25m
Museum of the City of New York — 103rd & Fifth
Halumii Ktapihna: Lenape Legacies and Futures opened Sep 25. The permanent Timescapes film is the best 30-minute primer on the city there is.
